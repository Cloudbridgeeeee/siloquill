# Product

SiloQuill: Smarter inventory planning for agricultural storage.

AI inventory planning software that helps agricultural storage businesses forecast storage requirements and identify inventory discrepancies.

- Founder and CEO: Musa Abdullahi
- Co-founder and CTO: Chidinma Okeke
- Industry: Agriculture, Supply Chain Management, Business Intelligence
- Customers: Grain warehouses, agricultural cooperatives and commodity distributors
- Core features: Storage demand forecasting, inventory reconciliation, batch movement analysis, capacity predictions, dispatch planning
- MVP: Inventory records, storage dashboards and capacity forecasts
- Planned AWS: SageMaker AI, Textract, DynamoDB, S3, Lambda
- Business model: Subscriptions based on storage locations
- Main action: Request a pilot
