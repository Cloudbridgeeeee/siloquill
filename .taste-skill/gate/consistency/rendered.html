How a SiloQuill pilot runs

  1. Import stock records and waybills

    You send two seasons of stock records and scanned waybills. We read them and match each to a batch and store.

  2. Weekly storage forecasts

    Each Monday a new 60-day forecast arrives, and we measure it against what was really stored.

  3. Reconcile and plan dispatch

    Managers review each gap and dispatch plan; every decision is logged.

Questions

What storage managers ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

What data do we need?

Stock records as spreadsheets or exports, plus waybills and delivery notes. Scanned paper is fine.

Does it change our stock records?

No. SiloQuill reads your records and suggests; a person makes every change.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by the number of storage locations. We quote after the pilot.

Can cooperatives with many small stores use it?

Yes. Each store is a location, and forecasts roll up across them.

Where is our data kept?

In an AWS account of its own, in the region you choose, encrypted at rest and in transit.
